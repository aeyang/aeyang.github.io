# Hello world 🚀
To be honest, this blog is more for me than it is for you. I once heard part of a quote which stuck with me: "...You don't know anything clearly unless you can state it in writing...". This is a forcing function for me to organize my thoughts which will ultimately reveal to me where the gaps in my understanding are. 

Additionally, clear writing is a skill in its own right. And in the age of AI, its importance may be even greater.
